Our Core Privacy Commitments

At Advanced Cyber Security Network (ACSN), we follow a strict Privacy-First approach to ensure a secure online experience for all visitors.

1. Zero Personal Data Tracking

We do not collect, track, or sell your personal information, browsing habits, or location data. Your online activity on ACSN remains private.

2. Client-Side Security Tools

All free security tools and calculators provided on ACSN run locally within your web browser. None of your input data is saved or processed on our servers.

3. Third-Party Links & Software

ACSN recommends verified security tools and software. However, visiting third-party links is subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Direct Communication Privacy

When you contact us via our support form or email, we only use your information to respond to your inquiry and never share it with third-party advertisers.

Data Handling & Security Standards

Detailed guidelines on how we handle website cookies, child privacy, and policy updates to maintain full transparency.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

ACSN uses minimal technical cookies or browser storage only to remember basic user preferences (like active themes or navigation) without tracking your identity.

6. Children's Online Privacy

Our content and educational tools are designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable data from children under the age of 13.

7. Information Security Measures

We employ modern web encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL) to protect your browsing session and ensure safe interaction across all sections of ACSN.

8. Privacy Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect changes in web security standards. Any revisions will be published here with immediate effect.
