🎯 What is this tool created for?

Primary Purpose: This Cyber Security & Privacy Awareness tool demonstrates how much sensitive hardware, battery, display, and network telemetry any standard website can collect about your device silently through your web browser — without requesting any special permissions. It helps users audit their browser tracking uniqueness and understand online fingerprinting exposure.
